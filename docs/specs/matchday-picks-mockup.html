The app ships with five themes, picked in Settings → Theme. Tap one to see it on the phone.

Design: "Floodlight" (one of the five themes)

A night pitch under floodlights. Dark green-black surfaces, one lime accent kept only for strong picks and money that comes back, coral for losses, amber for warnings. Headings in Bricolage Grotesque, body in Inter, every number in JetBrains Mono so odds line up. Tickets look like real betting slips with a torn, perforated edge.
